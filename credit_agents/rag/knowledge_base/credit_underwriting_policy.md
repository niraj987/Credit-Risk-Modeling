# Institutional Underwriting Guidelines & Credit Policy Manual

## 1. Credit Tier Classification & Cutoffs
Lending decisions and rate matrix classifications are partitioned into four quantitative tiers:

| Tier Name | FICO/Credit Score | Maximum Permissible DTI | Default Probability (PD) Band | Benchmark Action | Rate Spread (APR Over Base) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 (Prime)** | $\ge 720$ | $\le 36.0\%$ | $\le 10.0\%$ | Auto-Approve | Base + 2.00% |
| **Tier 2 (Near-Prime)** | $660 - 719$ | $\le 43.0\%$ | $10.1\% - 25.0\%$ | Conditional Approval / Standard Rate | Base + 4.25% |
| **Tier 3 (Subprime)** | $580 - 659$ | $\le 50.0\%$ | $25.1\% - 45.0\%$ | High Risk / Guarantor or Co-Signer Required | Base + 7.50% |
| **Tier 4 (Prohibited)** | $< 580$ or | $> 50.0\%$ | $> 45.0\%$ | Mandatory Rejection | N/A (Statutory Denial) |

## 2. Hard Policy Gates and Boundary Rules
1. **DTI Ceiling**: Total Debt-To-Income ($DTI$) cannot exceed 50.0% under any circumstance.
2. **Employment Stability**: Minimum continuous employment of 6 months. If employment $< 6$ months, approved only if applicant possesses a co-signer with credit score $\ge 700$.
3. **Maximum Exposure Limit**:
   - Single applicant unsecured limit: \$100,000.
   - For loan amounts $> \$50,000$, applicant credit score must be $\ge 680$ and DTI $\le 40\%$.
4. **Co-Signer Covenants**: A qualified co-signer cures Tier 3 subprime status if the co-signer's personal credit score is $\ge 700$ and household combined DTI is $\le 38\%$.
