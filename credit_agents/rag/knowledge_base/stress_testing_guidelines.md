# Stress Testing & Macroeconomic Scenario Framework (CCAR / DFAST)

## 1. Regulatory Stress Test Standards
Under the Dodd-Frank Act Stress Testing (DFAST) and Comprehensive Capital Analysis and Review (CCAR), institutions must subject loan applicants and loan books to macroeconomic stress shocks to determine portfolio resilience.

## 2. Standard Supervisory Scenarios
1. **Baseline Scenario**:
   - Federal funds rate remains within current projection band.
   - Unemployment rate steady ($\sim 4.0\%$).
   - PD multiplier: $1.0\times$ (no adjustment).

2. **Adverse Scenario (Stagflationary Pressure)**:
   - Inflation shock driving interest rates up by $+150$ basis points ($+1.5\%$).
   - Unemployment increases to $6.5\%$.
   - PD multiplier: $1.35\times$.

3. **Severely Adverse Scenario (Recessionary Crash)**:
   - Severe contraction in domestic economic activity.
   - Benchmark interest rates spiked by $+300$ basis points ($+3.0\%$) on floating debts.
   - Nationwide unemployment spikes to $10.0\%$.
   - PD multiplier: $1.80\times$.

## 3. Resilience Criteria for Conditional Approval
- An applicant classified in Tier 2 (Near-Prime) or Tier 3 (Subprime) whose Stressed PD under the *Adverse Scenario* exceeds $55.0\%$ must have mandatory loan covenants:
  - Loan term capped at maximum 36 months (to shorten credit horizon).
  - Escrow or debt-consolidation covenant required.
  - Recommended interest rate surcharge of $+125$ bps to compensate for credit cycle tail risk.
